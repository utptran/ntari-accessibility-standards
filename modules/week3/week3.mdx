I did not do the manual versus automated test, however I do know the takeaway message being conveyed.
The trade-off in this feud is time versus accuracy. Automation shortens auditing, but may miss certain
test rules or cannot do at all (incomplete). Manual auditing should be more accurate, but takes mroe time.

The blend between these two is crucial, as we're not aiming to fully automate or fully test manually.
Similar to the Janus-facing architecture concept, the goal in evolution of government versus the population
isn't to wipe out the competition, 'homeostatis, not harmony' or running with the competition is the main idea.

This is what auditing is about, let automation signal quick wins or bigger problems, and use the time
saved with your professional knowledge to create a more well-rounded Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR)
that technology cannot (e.g., purpose).
